## What it shows

1. **The desk did flag it, through INFO, 20 s after launch.** It was not a CALL: no rule has qualified.
2. **What triggered the INFO was a paid blue check, not reach.** `xRead` accepts `verified === true`
   (`calls.ts`, `established`), so a blue check bought for an account 84 minutes old with 64 followers counts the same
   as a large account. Here it worked. @Sir_Cat_solana (5 Oct) also had a paid individual check, and it was a launch
   that never posted its CA. The check alone is cheap to buy; the **own CA post 2 s after creation** is the strong
   part. F2 (`FLAG-LEDGER.md`) should split INFO by check type and account age.
3. **The gate cannot score coins like this.** The curve filled in 56 s. At phone speed the simulated buy lands at
   383 SOL, near graduation, and the simulation sells **at the graduation price** because the pool after graduation
   is not observed (`direct.ts`, "a curve that completes while held sells at the graduation price"). Every rule
   therefore records about −0.4 %, whatever the coin did on the pool afterwards. **For any coin that graduates
   within the hold, the gate cannot see what happened after graduation, and runners like this are among them.**
   R1–R3 (README, ranked this morning) would not catch this class either: at 120–420 s the coin had already
   graduated, so it is not simulated at all.
4. **Product launch, not a meme.** The coin is the token of a launch mechanic ("creator fees to any website"),
   announced an hour before with a "No Token Yet" post. That is the owner's "new exciting projects" category and a
   narrative tag of its own (`NARRATIVE-REGIME.md`: add `PRODUCT`).
5. **Heavy flipping in the first minute:** 315 SOL sold against 395 bought. Breadth was real (250 unique buyers, top
   wallet 7 % at 60 s), but so was the exit flow.
